Captures click-level events from the assessment and appointment-booking flows of the mental-health web app, for an enterprise client, replacing Google Analytics and Datadog RUM, which could not show per-step behaviour.

### Impact

Design and implementation of the whole backend from scratch, working with product, front-end, identity, DevOps and QA teams against a challenging launch date.
