Backend of the Global Intake Form in TELUS Health One, which lets employee-assistance members in countries without online scheduling request a confidential counselling appointment, in up to 60 languages. Each request is stored and routed to the Global Services Team.

### Impact

Design and implementation of the backend for the initial build and launch, working with product, QA and front-end teams. Live since 1 July 2025 in the EU, AU, CA and US, serving thousands of requests.
