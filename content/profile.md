Generalist software engineer and consultant with over a decade of experience designing, building and running cloud-native systems, with a preference for Python and AWS serverless but not limited to them. Works from the client's problem: gathers requirements, designs the architecture and records the decisions. Designs microservices to stay small, loosely coupled and independently deployable, and avoids the distributed monolith. Puts deliberate effort into both the development workflow (test-driven, peer- and AI-reviewed) and the deployment workflow (CI/CD), so quality is provable before anything reaches production. Observability is a first-class citizen: logs, metrics and traces break each business flow into steps that can be followed and scrutinised, and dashboards keep services and flows visible and under control. Above all, communicates clearly with stakeholders before, during and after delivery. Experienced with multi-tenant platforms and data residency constraints, with deployments across Europe, North America and Australia. AI is present at every stage of the engineering process. Comfortable owning services end to end, from design to production. Has lived and worked in the UK, Portugal and Angola, often in multicultural teams. Fluent in English and Portuguese.
